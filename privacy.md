# LevelWizard privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

LevelWizard is a field level book for survey and pipe crews, made by Zach Smith. This page
describes what the app does with your information. It is short because the app collects very little.

## What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter stays on your device:

- Job details (job name, crew, benchmark, design figures)
- Shot readings, and the elevations computed from them
- App preferences (the look, the appearance, the export style)

These are stored in the app's own storage on the phone. Nothing is sent to us, and there is no
account to create. There is no server for this app.

## What we collect

Nothing. LevelWizard has no analytics, no crash reporting service, no advertising, and no tracking
of any kind. The app does not use your location, your contacts, your camera, or your microphone, and
it asks for no permissions beyond the files you choose to share.

## Sharing and export

When you export a workbook, the app writes an Excel file and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where
that file goes is your choice: AirDrop, Mail, Files, or a message. We are not involved and receive
no copy.

## TestFlight

If you are testing a beta through TestFlight, Apple collects information about your use of TestFlight
itself, including crash reports, as described in Apple's own privacy policy. That is Apple's
collection, not ours. If you send us feedback by email, we keep that email and your address for as
long as it takes to answer you.

## Children

The app is a professional field tool and is not directed at children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Because the app stores everything
locally, a change here cannot affect data you have already recorded.
